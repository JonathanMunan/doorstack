Why this inventory is off-market

Developers cannot discount in public. They can discount in private.

A building with 60 units cannot have one sell for 10% under list on the MLS. The moment it does, every other unit in the building is worth less, and every buyer who already paid full price knows it.The developer's problem, in one sentence
  • Finished inventory costs money every month. Carrying costs, condo fees and financing on unsold units push developers to move volume quietly rather than list unit by unit.
  • A public price cut is permanent. Listing history is visible to every future buyer and appraiser. A private block sale is not.
  • Volume buyers solve the problem in one transaction. Four, eight or twelve units to one buyer clears inventory without touching the building's public pricing.
  • That is the trade. You take on multiple units at once. In exchange you get pricing a single retail buyer will never be offered.

Fit

Who this is for, and who it is not for.

A good fit

  • Investors who can take on four or more units in one purchase
  • Buyers with financing or capital already in place, or arranging it now
  • Landlords, corporate housing and furnished-rental operators expanding in Calgary
  • Family offices, investor groups and funds looking for scale without an MLS bidding war
  • Canadian citizens, permanent residents and Canadian-controlled corporations

Not a fit

  • Buyers looking for a single condo to live in or rent out
  • Anyone expecting to see addresses and prices before a conversation
  • Buyers who need a guaranteed return or a rental guarantee to proceed
  • Purchasers currently restricted under Canada's foreign-buyer rules

FAQ

Straight answers.

Why can't I see the buildings and prices on this site?

Because the developers need it that way. Public pricing below list would affect every other unit in their buildings. We share full details, in writing, once we know what you are looking for and that you can transact.

Are these new condos or resale?

Mostly developer-owned inventory: newly completed or recently completed units that were never sold, plus some developer-held rental stock. New units come with the developer's disclosure package and warranty coverage under Alberta's New Home Buyer Protection program, and GST applies to new units.

Do I have to buy the whole block from one building?

Usually the pricing is tied to volume in one building, because that is what solves the developer's problem. Some developers will price across two of their projects. We will tell you what is on the table.

Who pays you?

Commission is paid by the developer, as with any new-construction purchase. Before we collect any confidential information from you, we give you the written disclosure the Real Estate Council of Alberta requires: who we represent, how we are paid, and any conflicts of interest.

Can I use my own lawyer, lender and inspector?

Yes, and you should. We will coordinate with them and can refer professionals who handle multi-unit closings if you need names.

Is there a guaranteed rent or return?

No. We do not offer rental guarantees, projected returns or investment advice. We provide current rents in the building where the developer can share them and leave the underwriting to you and your advisors.
